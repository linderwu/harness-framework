## Ownership gate

[only explicit bound workflows may call guarded Hive repository writers](../../../tests/conversation-lifecycle-ownership.test.ts#L170) uses TypeScript AST symbol resolution against `lib/hive-memory/repository.ts`. Its exact 12-tuple allowlist covers only bound `ConversationService`/queue behavior, the named legacy dispatcher branch, and `createHiveServices` worker-handoff persistence. It may shrink, but may not grow without an approved architecture decision.
